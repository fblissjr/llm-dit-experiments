# LTX-2 Prompting Guide

Last updated: 2026-01-16

LTX-2 requires **detailed, paragraph-style prompts** (4-8 sentences) for optimal results. Short prompts produce poor output.

## Prompt Structure

Write prompts as a **single flowing paragraph** that includes:

1. **Shot establishment** - Camera angle, distance (close-up, medium, wide)
2. **Scene setting** - Location, lighting, atmosphere
3. **Subject details** - Appearance, position, characteristics
4. **Action/movement** - What's happening, how things move
5. **Camera movement** - Static, tracking, panning, zooming
6. **Audio/dialogue** (optional) - Ambient sounds, speech

## Key Rules

- **Length**: 4-8 descriptive sentences minimum
- **Tense**: Present tense for all movement and action
- **Style**: Cinematographic, like a screenplay or shot description
- **Detail**: Be specific about colors, textures, lighting, movement

## Bad vs Good Prompts

**Bad** (too short):
```
A dog in a park
```

**Good** (detailed):
```
A golden retriever runs joyfully through a sun-dappled park, its fur gleaming in the warm afternoon light. The camera tracks alongside as the dog bounds across lush green grass, tongue out and tail wagging. Birds chirp in the background as leaves rustle in a gentle breeze. The scene captures the pure happiness of a dog at play.
```

## Example Prompts by Category

### Text Rendering
```
A close-up shot of a white wall with the word HELLO painted in bold red letters. The camera slowly zooms in as warm afternoon sunlight casts soft shadows across the textured surface. The letters appear hand-painted with visible brush strokes. Dust particles float gently through the beam of light.
```

### Simple Objects
```
A medium shot of a bright red rubber ball resting on a pristine white surface. The lighting is soft and even, creating gentle shadows beneath the ball. The camera holds steady as dust particles float through a beam of sunlight. The ball's glossy surface reflects the ambient light with a subtle sheen.
```

### Animals
```
A golden retriever runs joyfully through a sun-dappled park, its fur gleaming in the warm afternoon light. The camera tracks alongside as the dog bounds across lush green grass, tongue out and tail wagging. Birds chirp in the background as leaves rustle in a gentle breeze. The scene captures the pure happiness of a dog at play.
```

### Complex Scenes
```
A bustling city street at night comes alive with neon signs reflecting off rain-slicked pavement. Crowds of people in dark coats hurry past storefronts while taxis honk in the distance. The camera slowly pans across the scene capturing the vibrant energy of urban nightlife. Steam rises from a nearby food cart as streetlights cast long shadows.
```

### Abstract/Artistic
```
A dreamlike surreal landscape unfolds with floating islands suspended in a pink and purple sky. Ethereal mist swirls around ancient stone structures as bioluminescent plants pulse with soft light. The camera drifts slowly through this otherworldly realm. Crystalline formations catch and refract light in rainbow patterns.
```
